About

I’m a UI/UX/ product/graphic designer with 15+ years of experience blending design, technology, and user research to create digital experiences that are useful and  intuitive to meet business and user goals.

My background in web development gives me a practical understanding of how digital products are built, while my experience in UX helps me focus on the people using them. I enjoy working through complex problems, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and turning ideas into experiences that make sense for real people.

When I’m not at my day job, you’ll find me behind a camera, volunteering with cat rescue, or teaching a cycling class. I’m naturally curious, always learning, and happiest when I’m solving a problem and making something better along the way.
