AI Chatbot - Mia

App design Artificial intelligence Usability testing Interviews

The goal of MIA was to help store team members quickly find the information they need without searching across multiple systems. The solution was an intuitive, centralized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot powered by the documents and resources team members use most, making answers easier and faster to find. I conducted in-store interviews after launch to watch how they used the product and gain insight into future enhancements. I also helped format documents so they could be broken apart for better retrieval.
