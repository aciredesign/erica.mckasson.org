Digital Production Planner

App design Interviews Workshop Usability testing

A co-creation event brought together business, technology, data and stores to develop ideas on how to improve accuracy of warmer items and reduce food waste. During this week, the team visited a store to see what their workflow entailed, and gathered insight to produce "how might we" statement to solve for accuracy and waste. The even was a huge success, in the end, the Digital Production Planner was born, helping stores to track their makes and stales in the kitchen. The app saved stores time from printing, hand writing, and hand keying in their makes and stales into a computer daily.
